多喜乐，长安宁。

A wish.
A name.
A way of being.

"May there be much joy, and lasting peace." CHANG'AN began as this wish. It became a person's name, then a way of returning to one's center.

The memory of clouds parting over dark mountains, of still water reflecting stone, of a quiet center held inside.

Six States of Being

多 · 喜 · 乐 · 长 · 安 · 宁

多

Duō · Abundance

Generosity of spirit, richness of material.

喜

Xǐ · Joy

The lightness of delight. A quiet smile in metal and stone.

乐

Lè · Harmony

Deep contentment where nothing is added and nothing missing.

长

Cháng · Longevity

Objects that age with grace. Crafted to be passed down.

安

Ān · Stillness

The center holds. Restrained design, the weight of presence.

宁

Níng · Peace

Forms that settle, a piece that feels like coming home.
