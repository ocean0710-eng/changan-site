Our Story

From a wish, to a name, to a way of being.

安

The name CHANG'AN comes from the Chinese blessing "多喜乐，长安宁" — may there be much joy and lasting peace. It became a person's name, then a way of holding one's center in a shifting world.

From the Mist, Into the World

Morning mist over dark mountains. Still water against stone. A quiet held inside. These are not symbols to decode — they are forms to wear. Every CHANG'AN piece translates a moment of stillness into proportion, weight, texture, and line.

Four Materials, One Approach

Turquoise, jade, precious metal, and fine wood — selected at source, worked by hand in our studio. We do not force the material. We listen to what it already is, then shape what it asks to become.

Six States, Six Ways to Wear Stillness

多喜乐长安宁. Each character names a state — abundance, joy, ease, endurance, grounded presence, stillness. Each piece belongs to one of these states. Together they form not a collection but a life.

安

Ān · Grounded Presence

The center holds. Restrained design, quiet surfaces, the weight of presence. Pieces for when you need to feel your feet on the ground.
