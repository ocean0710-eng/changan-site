多喜乐，长安宁。

A wish.
A name.
A way of being.

"May there be much joy, and lasting peace." CHANG'AN began as this wish. It became a person's name, then a way of returning to one's center.

The memory of clouds parting over dark mountains, of still water reflecting stone, of a quiet center held inside — these are not symbols to decode. They are forms translated into proportion, weight, texture, and line.

Six States of Being

多 · 喜 · 乐 · 长 · 安 · 宁

多

Duō · Abundance

Generosity of spirit, richness of material. Pieces that celebrate fullness.

喜

Xǐ · Joy

The lightness of delight. Bright forms, warm tones, a quiet smile in metal and stone.

乐

Lè · Harmony

Deep contentment. Balanced compositions where nothing is added and nothing is missing.

长

Cháng · Longevity

Objects that age with grace. Crafted to be passed down, not replaced.

安

Ān · Stillness

The center holds. Restrained design, quiet surfaces, the weight of presence.

宁

Níng · Peace

Resolution. Forms that settle, lines that conclude, a piece that feels like coming home.

Materials & Craft

Made to be felt.
Made to endure.

Four material families — gemstone, jade, precious metal, and fine wood — each selected at source, each worked by hand in our studio. No two stones, no two grains, no two pieces are identical. That is the point.

Gemstones

Turquoise from Hubei, Nanhong agate from Sichuan, each hand-selected for color and matrix

Jade

Hetian nephrite from Xinjiang, Burmese jadeite — carved to honor the stone's natural character

Precious Metals

925 sterling silver, 18K yellow gold, platinum 950 — forged, cast, and finished by hand

Fine Wood

Golden Nánmù, wild agarwood, ebony — air-dried, hand-carved, never lacquered over the grain
